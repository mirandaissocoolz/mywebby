My Story

2026

I first got into photography after a family trip to Cuba. I still remember the entire plane ride home experimenting with Apple's built-in photo filters, like Vivid and Vivid warm (yea...) Before long, I was convincing my dad to buy me the Canon G7X Mark ii (which I still use today).

Years later, I joined a media team, where I learned photo editing. Today, I still love taking photos on family vacations (lol) and experimenting with cool photo ideas whenever possible.
